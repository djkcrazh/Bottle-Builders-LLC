Three ways in

01

Schedule waste pickups

We collect from households, schools and businesses through the GREENCO app, and run collection events that bring communities in. If you have plastic sitting somewhere, book a pickup and we will come and get it. You get paid for it.

02

Join the crew

Become a Green Agent through the GREENCO app and earn by filling pickup orders in your own vehicle. Schools, NGOs and community groups run collection programs with us too. If you want to work on the collection network or in the facility, write to us.

03

Build with us

We sell concrete masonry units, pavement and building materials strengthened with recycled plastic flakes, priced against cement cost and demand. We also sell flakes, bales and pellets to industrial buyers.
