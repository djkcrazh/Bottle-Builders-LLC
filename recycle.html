Detail 01  /  Green Agents

Drive to collect waste.
Get paid.

The pickups people book have to be driven by somebody. That somebody is a Green Agent, and it can be you.

Green Agents register through the GREENCO app and earn by filling pickup orders. Open orders show up near you, you collect the plastic from the household, school or business that posted it, and you are paid for every order you complete. Any vehicle works: a car, a van, a tricycle or a motorbike. You drive when you want to, and take as many orders as you can carry. To register, contact us and sign up through the app.

Step 01

Register

Get in touch, then create your Green Agent account in the app.

Step 02

Take orders

Open pickup orders near you, claimed on your own schedule.

Step 03

Drive

Collect the plastic in whatever vehicle you have available.

Step 04

Get paid

Earn on every order you complete, paid straight to you.

Detail 02  /  Sequence

How a pickup works

Four steps, and the last one is the reason the other three matter.

  1. Open GREENCO

    Register as a household, a school, a business or a collector.

  2. Book a pickup

    Say what you have and where it is. A Green Agent comes to you.

  3. Get paid

    You are paid for what you hand over, at a rate nobody skims.

  4. It gets built

    Your plastic is shredded, logged in KATAROS, and pressed into blocks.

03
Detail 03  /  Downstream

Where it ends up

Handing plastic to us is not the same as handing it to a recycler who sells it on. Yours comes back as a building material.

Then

Shredded

Bottles become flakes, and the intake is logged against your pickup.

Then

Blended

Flakes are mixed with plant biomasses.

Then

Built

The mix is pressed into concrete masonry units and sent out to sites.
